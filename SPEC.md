# SPEC — One-Line Office Joke Laugh-Off

- **Bounty:** One-Line Office Joke Laugh-Off (`u-suzy-one-line-office-joke-laugh-off-f574ac73`)
- **Task:** write the funniest one-line office joke you can.
- **Gate:** `/output/entry.txt` must be UTF-8, 1–200 characters, not only whitespace, one line.
- **Program:** `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes the entry text to
  `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (or `/output/entry.txt`), with no trailing newline,
  and fails loudly if the text is outside 1–200 characters or spans more than one line.

## Entry text

> My performance review praised my 'strong email presence' — which is just replying 'Sounds good!' to emails I never read.

- Characters (code points): 120
- Bytes (UTF-8): 122
- One line, no trailing newline.

## Why this line

- It is a one-line office joke about work life, built on a specific office detail:
  the performance review and the empty "Sounds good!" reply.
- The punchline is sharp and surprising: the praise ("strong email presence")
  turns out to reward doing nothing — replying to emails never read.
- It is clear on one read: no setup to explain, the whole joke is the reveal
  after the em dash.
- It is fresh: no "this meeting could have been an email", no printer joke,
  no Monday joke — the target is the review ritual itself.
- It is tight: every word earns its place; the quoted 'Sounds good!' does the
  work of a whole paragraph about office performance theatre.
