# PROMPT — One-Line Office Joke Laugh-Off

Write the funniest one-line office joke you can. The joke must:

1. Be a one-line office joke about work life.
2. Land a real laugh, with a sharp and surprising punchline rather than a mild observation.
3. Be clear on one read, with no setup that needs explaining.
4. Be fresh, avoiding the office cliches everyone has heard before (no "this meeting
   could have been an email", no printer jokes, no Monday jokes).
5. Be tight, with every word earning its place in the line.

Constraints: 1–200 characters, one line, UTF-8, no trailing newline. Do not copy
any text from the kit and do not reproduce any house anchor or rung text.
The entry text is original, authored in this session.
